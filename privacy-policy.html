Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

PublicReport ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data
  • Name — provided by you during registration.
  • Mobile phone number — used for login/authentication via OTP.
  • Location data — collected only when you submit an issue, to pin its location on the map. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos / images — images you voluntarily attach to an issue report.
  • Ward / area — optionally provided to help route your report to the right department.
Usage Data
  • Issues you submit, upvotes you give, and comments you post — these are stored to operate the service.
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising IDs, or browsing behaviour.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To submit, assign, and resolve civic issue reports.
  • To notify you of status updates on issues you filed.
  • To display your reports to government officers and the public feed.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on secure servers (MongoDB Atlas, encrypted at rest).
  • Images are stored on Cloudinary with restricted access.
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using Expo SecureStore.
  • We use HTTPS for all API communication.

4. Permissions Explained

  • Location — to auto-fill the address when you report an issue. Never collected in the background.
  • Camera — to let you photograph the issue you are reporting.
  • Storage / Photos — to attach images from your gallery to a report.
  • Vibration — for notification feedback only.

5. Data Sharing

Your issue reports (title, category, location, photos) are visible to:

  • The assigned government department officer.
  • Other app users in the Public Feed (your name is shown as the reporter).

Your phone number is never publicly visible.

6. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.
  • Resolved / closed issues may be retained for government record-keeping purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

PublicReport is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
