── FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max) ────────────

PublicReport is your direct link to local government — report roads, water,
electricity, garbage, drainage, and street-light issues in seconds and track
every step of the resolution.

🏙️ REPORT IN SECONDS
• Pick the issue category (Road, Water, Electricity, Garbage, Drainage, Street Light)
• Add a title, description, and up to 4 photos
• Auto-detect your location with one tap
• Set priority — Low, Medium, High, or Critical

📍 REAL-TIME TRACKING
• Every report gets a unique Ticket ID (e.g. CR-ROA-00001)
• Live status timeline: Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved
• Track any complaint by Ticket ID without logging in

🗳️ COMMUNITY FEED
• Browse all issues in your city
• Upvote problems affecting your neighbourhood to push them up the priority queue
• Leave comments and follow updates

🌐 AVAILABLE IN 3 LANGUAGES
• English  |  हिंदी  |  తెలుగు
• Switch language anytime from the login screen or profile

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• Your phone number is never publicly visible
• Location is captured only when you submit a report — never tracked in background
• No ads, no data selling

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your mobile number
2. Tap "Report an Issue" and fill in the details
3. Submit — your report is instantly assigned to the right government department
4. Get notified when the status changes
5. Issue resolved? Rate the resolution!

PublicReport bridges the gap between citizens and local government. Every report
you submit helps make your city cleaner, safer, and better connected.
